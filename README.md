## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Monster Girls: You Can't Say No 🔞**. This trainer provides control over the game's main progression systems, including incubus heat, money, relationship progress, ability upgrades, schedule management, inventory quantities, and interaction mini-games.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the adult visual novel's campus, relationships, interactive dialogue, incubus progression, and story outcomes with less resource management.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Incubus Heat** — Prevents the incubus heat meter from rising while enabled.
- **Reduce Incubus Heat** — Lowers the current heat level to help maintain control and avoid losing progress.
- **Unlimited Money** — Sets the available money to a high value for purchases and campus activities.
- **Freeze Time** — Stops the in-game schedule from advancing while exploring the campus and managing daily activities.
- **Flirting Mini-Game Score** — Adds points during dialogue-based flirting encounters, helping reach the required threshold for special scenes.

### 📌 Progression

- **Relationship Progress** — Adjusts relationship progress with supernatural characters to make new interactions and scenes easier to access.
- **Incubus Ability Points** — Provides ability points for upgrading the incubus and unlocking additional possibilities.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Inventory Quantities** — Sets supported inventory items to a high quantity for quests and interactions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal controls to navigate available campus locations and move between story areas. Activate Freeze Time before exploring when you want to plan activities without advancing the daily schedule.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the in-game inventory to review available items. Enable Inventory Quantities before using supported items for quests or interactions, then refresh the inventory screen to confirm the updated values.

### World & Environment

Attend classes, explore the fantasy college, meet supernatural characters, start quests, use the phone, and manage the in-game time and heat meter. Money, relationship progress, and ability points can be adjusted before beginning important activities.

### Combat & Weapons

The game does not use a conventional combat system. Its main survival pressure comes from managing incubus heat, making relationship choices, completing interactions, and avoiding progress loss. Use the heat options during story sections where the meter becomes difficult to maintain.
